Track Passing Rules & Tolerances

check_circle Zero foot dab on tarmac or ground
check_circle Maintain continuous rolling 1st gear speed (8-12 km/h)
check_circle Never clip white border optical kerbs
check_circle Complete exactly two full loops in figure-8 trajectory

Step-by-Step Sensor Navigation Protocol

  1. 1
    Approach entry lane: Align vehicle along the left boundary line at precisely 10 km/h in 1st gear. Avoid clutch dragging.
  2. 2
    Left steering lock: Begin gradual left lock approximately half a car-length before the central crossover junction.
  3. 3
    Center crossover counter-steer: Counter-steer smoothly across the intersection point without sudden braking to clear the laser margin.
  4. 4
    Right loop & exit: Complete the right steering arc maintaining constant accelerator pressure, straightening up before the optical exit barrier.

Track Passing Rules & Tolerances

check_circle Single forward adjustment permitted per parking bay

Step-by-Step Sensor Navigation Protocol

  1. 1
    Entry Bay Drive-In: Drive straight forward into the left lane bay (Bay A). Bring vehicle to a complete square stop without touching front stop kerb.
  2. 2
    Reverse corridor entry: Shift cleanly into reverse gear, inspect left blind-spot, and begin rolling reverse straight until rear tyres clear the pole line.
  3. 3
    Bay C curve docking: Steer sharply into the opposite transverse bay in a single uninterrupted curve, keeping mirror clearance at 25cm.
  4. 4
    Parallel exit: Realign wheels to true parallel, verify clear sensors, and drive forward out through Bay D into test clearance zone.
