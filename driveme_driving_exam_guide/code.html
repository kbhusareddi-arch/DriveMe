RTO Exam Day Checklist

Mandatory documentation and physical vehicle requirements before entering the testing bay.

verified DigiLocker Verified

badge Documents to Carry

  • check_box Learner's License (LL): Original physical copy or digital ID with barcode.
  • check_box Form 5 / 5B Certificate: Authorized driving training certificate from motor school.
  • check_box Slot Booking Receipt: Paid fee receipt and Sarathi portal confirmation slip.

directions_car Vehicle Inspection Ready

  • check_box Dual-Control SUV/Hatch: Valid RC, active comprehensive insurance & PUCC certificate.
  • check_box Red 'L' Board: Prominently affixed at front and rear in accordance with CMVR rules.
  • check_box Working Lights & Horn: Hazard indicators, brake light, reverse horn functional.

person_check Candidate Protocol

  • check_box Appropriate Footwear: Closed driving shoes mandatory. Slippers/heels lead to disqualification.
  • check_box Seatbelt Fastening: Must wear seatbelt before inserting key/starting the engine.
  • check_box Reporting Window: Check-in at automated biometric booth 30 mins prior to slot.
